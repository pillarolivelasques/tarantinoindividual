THE
EXPERIENCE

An immersive exhibition dedicated to the iconic world of Quentin Tarantino. Through his most celebrated films, original props, and behind-the-scenes material, visitors will step inside the cinematic universe of one of the most visionary directors of our time.

Get Your Tickets
01

Film Rooms

Each of Tarantino's nine films has its own dedicated room. Immersive environments built around iconic scenes, dialogue loops, and original costume replicas.

02

Original Props

Authentic production materials on loan from private collections — scripts, storyboards, and objects directly tied to the making of films like Pulp Fiction and Kill Bill.

03

Screening Room

A 40-seat cinema showing curated short films and documentary footage about Tarantino's creative process, influences, and cinematic obsessions.

04

Photo Zones

Step into the world of your favourite film. Recreated sets from Pulp Fiction's Jack Rabbit Slim's diner and Reservoir Dogs warehouse — available for photos and videos.

05

The Archive

A deep-dive library section with books, posters, and reference material curated directly from Tarantino's personal influences — from spaghetti westerns to Hong Kong martial arts cinema.

06

Shop & Bar

Exclusive merchandise designed for the exhibition, alongside a themed bar inspired by the films. Named after iconic drinks and characters from the Tarantinoverse.

"I steal from every single movie ever made. If my work has anything, it's that I'm taking this from this and that from that and mixing them together."

Find Us

The Tarantino Experience is hosted at the Victoria and Albert Museum in London — one of the world's greatest museums of art, design, and performance. The exhibition runs throughout 2026.

Open Tuesday to Sunday, 10:00–20:00. Late nights on Fridays until 22:00.
