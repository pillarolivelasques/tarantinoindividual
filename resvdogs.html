Resrvoir Dogs

1992 · 1h 35m · HD

A group of criminals, each using color-based aliases, are brought together by a crime boss to carry out a diamond heist. The robbery goes horribly wrong, and the surviving members suspect that one of them is an undercover police informant. As paranoia grows, the warehouse where they regroup becomes a tense space of mistrust, violence, and shifting alliances while they try to figure out who betrayed them.
