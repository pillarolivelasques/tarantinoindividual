Once Upon Time in Hollywood

2019 · 2h 49m · HD

The story takes place in 1969 Los Angeles and follows Rick Dalton, a fading TV actor, and his stunt double Cliff Booth, who are struggling to find work as Hollywood changes around them. At the same time, actress Sharon Tate is rising to fame, representing the bright future of the film industry. As Rick and Cliff try to stay relevant in Hollywood, their lives slowly intersect with the real-life Manson Family cult living in the city. The film builds toward a rewritten version of history where these paths collide in an unexpected way.
