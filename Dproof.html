Death Proof

2007 · 1h 45m · HD

Death Proof follows a psychopathic stuntman, “Stuntman Mike,” who uses his heavily reinforced car to stalk and kill women on the road, believing he is “death-proof” in his vehicle. After his first group of victims, he targets a new set of women—confident, sharp, and unaware of his true intentions. But this time, the tables turn when he picks the wrong group to mess with.
