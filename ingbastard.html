Inglourious Basterds

2009 · 2h 33m · HD

Inglourious Basterds is set in Nazi-occupied France during World War II, where two separate plots of revenge unfold. A group of Jewish-American soldiers known as “The Basterds” carry out brutal attacks against Nazis, while a young Jewish woman, Shosanna Dreyfus, secretly plans revenge after surviving the massacre of her family. Their paths eventually converge at a Nazi film premiere, where Shosanna prepares a deadly trap to strike at the heart of the Third Reich. The story mixes tension, dark humor, and alternate-history revenge.
