Kill Bill | The Whole Bloody Affair

2003 · 4h 35m · HD

Kill Bill: The Whole Bloody Affair follows The Bride, a former assassin who wakes up from a four-year coma after being brutally attacked on her wedding day by Bill and the Deadly Viper Assassination Squad. Determined to get revenge, she travels across the world to hunt down each member of the group one by one before finally confronting Bill himself. Combining Kill Bill Vol. 1 and Vol. 2 into a single film, the story blends intense action, martial arts, samurai cinema, and Quentin Tarantino’s signature style and dark humor.
