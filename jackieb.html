Jackie Brown

1997 · 2h 34m · HD

Jackie Brown is about a flight attendant who gets caught smuggling money for an arms dealer. When police pressure her to help them, she secretly plans a double-cross to keep the money for herself. With the help of a bail bondsman, she carefully manipulates both the police and the criminals, especially her dangerous employer Ordell Robbie. The story revolves around her risky plan to outsmart everyone and escape with the cash.
