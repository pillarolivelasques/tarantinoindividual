Django Unchained

2012 · 2h 45m · HD

Django Unchained is a Western drama directed by Quentin Tarantino. The film follows Django, a freed enslaved man who joins forces with a bounty hunter named Dr. King Schultz. Together, they hunt criminals across the South while Django searches for his wife, Broomhilda, who is held by the ruthless plantation owner Calvin Candie. The movie explores themes of freedom, revenge, and justice through intense action and dark humor.
