The Hateful Eight

2015 · 2h 48m · HD

The Hateful Eight is set in post–Civil War Wyoming, where a group of strangers becomes stranded in a remote cabin during a blizzard. Among them are bounty hunters, fugitives, and questionable lawmen, each hiding secrets and unclear intentions. As the storm traps them together, tension builds and trust quickly breaks down. Conversations turn into accusations, and it becomes clear that many of them are not who they claim to be.
